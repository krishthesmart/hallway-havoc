# Arcade Alley

Pick from **100 original browser arcade games** across ten styles. Every game has a live Nova opponent, and every run is a five-level ladder: win a round to reach a faster, trickier stage, earn bonus points, and become an **Arcade Champion**. Nova levels up with you. Choose **Chill**, **Sharp**, or **Wild** for the starting difficulty.

- **Paddle:** Neon Volley, Turbo Table, Curveball Club, Twin Spark, Gravity Smash, Zero-G Rally, Pinball Panic, Power Serve, Spin City, Last Paddle.
- **Targets:** Tiny Targets, Quickdraw Circuit, Orbit Blaster, Shield Break, Pop Shot, Bullseye Bash, Moving Marker, Glass Cannon, Rapid Pop, Golden Run.
- **Obstacle racing:** Roof Hop Relay, Rocket Lanes, Zigzag Circuit, Crate Escape, Afterburn Run, Coin Rush Run, Switchback Sprint, Meteor Dash, Neon Hurdles, Last Lap. Dodge lane blockers, jump barriers, duck under arches, and chain boost pickups.
- **Arena:** Bounce Blitz, Neon Dodgeball, Magnet Mayhem, Triple Threat, Ricochet Rumble, Comet Combat, Bubble Brawl, Hot Potato Bot, Pinball Punks, Last Bot Standing.
- **Hoops:** Skyline Hoops, Orbit Basket, Bumper Basket, Clutch Countdown, Rapid Fire, Bank Shot Bash, Moving Rim, Longshot League, Double Dunk, Buzzer Rush.
- **Memory:** Color Clash, Beat Battle, Blink Blitz, Pattern Panic, Final Echo, Mirror Match, Double Tap Memory, Tempo Tangle, Color Carousel, Perfect Recall.
- **Swinging:** Skyline Swing, Rope Rush, Hookshot Heights, Cloud Chaser, Nova's Gauntlet, Moonshot Lines, Rooftop Ropes, Hook and Fly, Skyhook Sprint, Last Anchor.
- **Platforming:** Trapdoor Dash, Spike Sprint, Gap Jumper, Hazard Hop, Skyline Gauntlet, Crumbly Crossing, Sawtooth Run, Wallkick Alley, Rooftop Rumble, Perfect Landing. Clear gaps and moving saws, then snag speed orbs for a burst.
- **Physics driving:** Dune Dash, Bumpy Boulevard, Ridge Racer, Rollover Rally, Nova's Hill Climb, Canyon Hop, Wobble Wheels, Rocket Ramp, Gravel Grinder, Big Air Derby. Bounce over ramps, avoid rocks and slick patches, control your airtime, and grab boosts.
- **Dodge survival:** Meteor Shower, Lava Pop, Neon Rain, Block Party, Comet Alley, Skyfall Shuffle, Asteroid Disco, Laser Drizzle, Chaos Confetti, Last One Up.

## Play

Choose a style filter and game, pick Nova's starting difficulty, and hit **Play**. Use the start card for each game's controls; on-screen buttons support touch. Press **Escape** to leave a run, **R** to retry the current level, and **Enter** or **Space** to advance after a level win. A loss retries that level, while **Game Select** returns to the arcade.

Races reward quick lane changes and well-timed jumps; the driving tracks reward throttle control and steady landings. Gold coins add points, while glowing pickups give a short speed boost. In the runner, **S/↓** ducks under overhead gates; in the driver, **W/S** tilts the car while airborne.

Jump straight into the rotating **Daily Showdown** (the same game and Sharp Nova difficulty for everyone each UTC day), or choose **Surprise Me** for an instant random game. Your daily best and win streak are saved locally in your browser. Share a result to invite a friend to the same game; results and scores are not uploaded to a global leaderboard.

Best scores are saved in your browser. Original procedural canvas art and synthesized audio mean there are no installs, accounts, or external assets.
