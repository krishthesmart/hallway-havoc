# Hallway Havoc

The bell rang. The snacks are loose. How long can you survive?

Play the live game: **https://krishthesmart.github.io/hallway-havoc/**

## How to play

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. On a phone or tablet, drag on the playfield.
- Grab snacks to score points. Keep collecting for bigger combos.
- Dodge runaway backpacks, skateboards, brooms, and books. You get three lives.
- Tap **Space** for a short sugar rush. Snacks are worth double points during the rush.
- Survive for 60 seconds to win. Your personal best is saved in your browser.

No installs, accounts, or downloads needed. Have fun!
